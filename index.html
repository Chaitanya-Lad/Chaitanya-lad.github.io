01 The bridge role

I make sure the right thing gets built — then prove that it works.

My work lives in the gap between what a device needs to do and what the code actually does. At STRUmed I'm the primary liaison between R&D and development — translating clinical and hardware intent into BRD, FRD, SRS, and DRS the team can build against, then holding the result to those specs.

I came up through hardware and Python, so requirements aren't abstract to me. I've written the sensor-acquisition modules, shaped the GUIs clinicians touch, and run the validation protocols that decide whether an IoT-enabled medical device is trustworthy enough to ship.

That dual vantage — analyst and engineer — is the point. I can sit with a stakeholder in the morning and a defect log by the afternoon, and keep both honest to the same source of truth.
